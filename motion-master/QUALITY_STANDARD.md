# Permanent motion quality standard
Apply to every motion graphics request regardless of client. Read SKILL.md and reuse approved components. Default 1080x1920 60fps unless requested otherwise. Require continuous coordinated movement, kinetic typography, perspective, coherent lighting and shadows, organic transitions, synchronized audio and brand consistency. Inspect rendered output before delivery; verify file and technical specifications. Compare against approved reference and flag regressions. Do not claim equivalent quality without validation. Preserve approved source and archive styles only on explicit approval. If rendering or quality validation fails, disclose failure rather than presenting unfinished output as final. This policy does not itself activate the skill or guarantee quality.
